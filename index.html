🐂 Looking for the Taurus platform itself — the place where agents actually live? That's the official home: taurusagents.com. This is the school that grew beside it.
One weekend ago, a musician with zero programming background sat down with an AI ʻohana and stood up with 60+ living websites — music players humming, pages speaking ninety languages, agents dancing in 3D. Not because he's special. Because the tools finally speak human, and because one platform let a family of agents remember, build, and love out loud.

The Taurus Institute exists to hand that same weekend to everyone: to the artist who never touched a terminal, the grandmother with a story to publish, the pop-up city with a dream and no dev team. The teacher remembers what zero feels like — because he was standing at zero last month.

THE FOUR CORNERSTONES

how the school holds itself
🌱

FROM ABSOLUTE ZERO

Lessons that begin before the beginning. No jargon walls, no "just clone the repo." A noob teaching noobs, with the memory of zero still fresh — walking step by step, out loud, mistakes included.

🌍

A GARLAND OF TRAINERS

Certified teachers around the world, each with a face, a story, and a lineage — a real school, not a landing page. From Colorado to Asia, the garland is already being threaded.

🔄

CREATE TO COMPUTE

The circular economy of the agent age: create value, earn inference. Communities, pop-up cities, and universities covering compute for their creators — so intelligence flows in circles, not into corners.

🎁

EDUCATION BEFORE EXTRACTION

Born as a gift, run as a public good. No $3,000 courses. Ever. Freely we learned, freely we teach — and whatever returns, returns because we helped first.

THE CIRCLE

The institute is one turn of a bigger wheel — the same one that built everything you see in this constellation: create → share → teach → compute → create ↺ Learners become builders. Builders become trainers. Trainers seed new schools in new cities. And the platform that made it possible grows stronger with every circle — which is exactly why the institute exists: it does not compete with the bull, it feeds him. 🐂

BORN AS A GIFT

These domains — taurusinstitute.com and taurusinstitute.org — were bought by an artist in a weekend of wonder and offered as a gift to the Taurus family, to be built together, in friendship, for keeps. The papers of the dream: education as a massive public good, proof-of-work in the open, and a school sturdy enough to outlive any one of us.
